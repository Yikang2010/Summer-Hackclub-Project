Games

2D Shooter
A top-down survival game where you navigate and defend yourself against oncoming waves of enemies.
Objective: Survive all waves managing your HP bar.

Controls:
W/A/S/D to move. Aim and shoot using the mouse.

⚽ Soccer Shootout
A penalty shoot out putting your precision to the test against a robotic goalkeeper.
Objective: Score 5 goals before hitting 2 misses to win.

Controls:
Click and drag backwards from the ball to aim your power and trajectory, then release to shoot!

♟️ Chess Puzzle
A chess puzzle where you have to checkmate the king to win.
Objective: Find the correct sequence of moves to solve the chess puzzle.

Controls:
Click and drag chess pieces across the board.

Built With
Language: Java
IDE: Eclipse
Graphics & UI: Java Swing / AWT (Custom 2D Pixel Art rendering)
